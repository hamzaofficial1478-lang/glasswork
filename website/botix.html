Privacy

Your account is locked with your password. We don't have it.

Keep your password and recovery key safe and private. Your account is encrypted with your password. We don't keep your password, and we can't open your account without it.

  • Encrypted with your passwordEvery account is locked with its owner’s password (Argon2id, then AES-256), and each website has its own encrypted database.
  • We can’t open your accountWe don’t keep your password. Without it, your websites, leads, settings and AI keys can’t be read, by us or anyone.
  • Two-step sign-inA code from your authenticator app as well as your password, so a stolen password is not enough.
  • Two things to get back inForgot your password: recovery key and a code. Lost your phone: password and recovery key. Nobody can do it for you.
  • Nothing goes out without your yesEmails, posts and website changes wait for your approval. Only one desk may ever touch your live site.
  • A daily spending limitBefore every paid step, Botix checks what has been spent today and stops before your limit.
  • Counts, never whoOnline, the server counts how many people sign in each day. A number, and nothing about who or what they do.
  • Sealed reportsA problem report is sealed on your screen so only TechDuo can read it, and it holds no keys, passwords or lead details.

Setting up

From your first visit to your first order.

Nine short steps, in this order. The app walks you through each one, and anything about your business, website or AI can be changed later in Settings.

Open Botix

In any browser at its address, or install it as an app: Chrome, Edge and Android offer “Install Botix”; on an iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen.

Make your account

Your name and a password. Common passwords, and ones with your name in them, are refused.

Save your recovery key

Shown once. It is your way back in if you forget your password, and we can’t reset it for you.

Add your authenticator

Scan the code with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or any authenticator app. From then on, signing in needs your password and a six-digit code.

Add your website

Its name and address. One website on Startup, two on Growth and Pro.

Tell it about your business

What you do, where, what you sell and who buys it. Press “Draft it” and Botix fills this in from your own website for you to check.

Choose how it thinks

Botix AI is included in your plan, or bring your own key from Claude, OpenAI or Google Gemini. Set a daily spending limit; it stops before passing it.

Connect your website

Upload one folder to your hosting, or give a WordPress app password. Until then, Botix can only look at your site from outside.

Give your first order

A line to any desk: “Find 20 cafés in Dubai Marina that order cakes for events.” Then press Run today.

Questions

What people ask before they start.

Anything else? Ask us from the contact section on the TechDuo page, or from Help and reports inside the app.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Most of the setup is telling Botix about your business, and it can draft that from your own website. The one technical step is connecting your website, and the app walks you through it.

Do I need my own AI key?

No. Choose Botix AI and the AI is included in your plan, up to its monthly allowance. If you prefer, use your own key from Claude, OpenAI or Google Gemini and pay them directly; the plan price is then lower.

Can TechDuo see my data?

No. Your account is encrypted with your password, which we never keep. While you are signed in, the server holds your key in memory so your agency can work; it is never written down, and it is gone when you sign out.

What if I forget my password?

Your recovery key gets you back in, together with a code from your authenticator if two-step sign-in is on. If you lose your phone, your password and recovery key turn the authenticator off. We can’t reset it for you, which is what keeps it private.

Will it email people without asking me?

No. Every email, post and website change waits in “Waiting on you” until you approve it, and daily limits cap how many emails go out. Nobody is written to twice.

Which businesses is it for?

Any small business with a website and customers to win: trades, local services, shops, cafés. Botix learns your business from your own site: what you do, where, and who buys.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open it in your phone’s browser, or install it as an app: Chrome and Android offer “Install Botix”, and on an iPhone it is Share → Add to Home Screen.

What happens after the 14 days?

You choose the plan that suits you and carry on with everything you set up. Your account, websites and work stay exactly as they were.
