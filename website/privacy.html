Privacy

What we know about you, and what we don't.

Draft for review. This notice has not yet been checked by a lawyer, and our contact details are added before launch.

This website

This website has no account, no form, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. We don't know who visits it.

  • The page fonts come from Google Fonts, and the 3D drawing code comes from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Like any website your browser visits, they see your internet address and the kind of browser you use. We receive nothing from them.
  • To show Botix prices in your own currency, the pricing section works out your country from your device's time zone and language setting, on your device; nothing is sent for that. It then loads today's exchange rates, a public file of numbers, from jsDelivr. That request carries nothing about you beyond what any web request does, and no address of the page you were on.
  • The company that hosts this website may keep ordinary access logs (internet address, time and page) for security, as web hosts do.
  • If you write to us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and we delete them when the conversation is over unless you are a customer.

Botix, in short

Botix is the product this website describes. It has its own, fuller privacy notice inside the app, which you can read before you make an account. In short:

  • Your account is encrypted with your password. We don't keep your password, and we can't open your account without it. Your websites, leads, settings and AI keys are stored so that nobody without your password can read them, TechDuo included.
  • While you are signed in online, the key that opens your data is held in the server's memory so your agency can work. It is never written down, and it is gone when you sign out.
  • Two-step sign-in asks for a code from your authenticator app as well as your password. If you forget your password, your recovery key and a code get you back in; if you lose your phone, your password and recovery key do. Nobody can do either for you.
  • Counts, never who. Online, the server counts how many people sign in each day. That number is all it reports. A copy on your own computer counts nothing.
  • Problem reports reach us only when you choose to send one, sealed on your screen so only we can read it. They hold no keys, passwords or lead details.
  • Other services receive information only when you use a feature that needs them, and only what that feature needs: the AI company you chose, your email provider for an email you approved, Google when you connect Search Console, your own website for changes you approved.

The people you contact with Botix

When Botix finds businesses for you to contact, you decide what is done with their details, so you are responsible for them under UAE data protection law. Botix helps: it prefers a business's published role address (such as info@), never writes to the same contact twice, and lets you delete what you no longer need. Stop contacting anyone who asks you to, and delete their details if they ask.

Children

Botix is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

If this notice changes, the date below changes with it. To ask about it, use the contact section on our home page, or Help and reports inside Botix.
