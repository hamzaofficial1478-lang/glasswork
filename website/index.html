How we work

Four rules we build by.

Built from real work

Botix grew out of running the online side of a working trade business in the UAE. Every screen answers a question a busy owner actually asks.

You stay in charge

Our software prepares the work and waits. Emails, posts and website changes go out only when you approve them.

Private by design

Your account is encrypted with your password. We don't keep your password, and we can't open your account without it.

Plain words

No jargon and no long contracts. You see what it did, what it found and what it cost, in words you would use yourself.

About TechDuo

A small company that builds the tools it wished it had.

TechDuo is our first company. It is run by someone with more than ten years in the trades, who knows how hard it is to find customers, keep a website up to date and answer every enquiry while also doing the work.

Botix is what came out of that. It does the routine online work a small business never has time for, and brings the owner only what needs a decision. We are starting with Botix and will build more tools for working businesses the same way: useful first, private always, and nothing done behind your back.
