GLASSWORK — TERMS OF USE (draft for review)

These terms say plainly how the program works and what it expects of you. They
have not yet been checked by a lawyer. The full version is in the program, under
Settings -> Terms of use.

WHAT THIS IS
A program you install and run on your own computer. It runs the online growth of
your business with a team of AI agents - finding leads, writing pages and posts,
looking after your website and your place on Google - and asks you before
anything leaves your computer.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Your account, your websites and everything the agency records are kept on your
computer, encrypted with your password. The publisher does not receive them.
Keep your recovery key safe: without your password and your recovery key, your
data cannot be opened again by anyone.

WHAT THE PUBLISHER DOES RECEIVE
- When the program looks for updates: a random number for your copy, its
  version and the kind of computer, so copies in use can be counted. You can
  switch this off in Help and reports; updates are still looked for.
- Only if you choose to register: the name, business, email, phone, website
  and country you type. You can withdraw it at any time.
- Only if you send a problem report: what you wrote and, if you tick the box,
  a support report with every key, password, address and number taken out.

SERVICES YOU CONNECT
The AI company, Google, maps, email and your website are reached with your own
keys. Their terms and prices apply, and you pay them directly. The daily
spending limit is a safeguard, not a promise about any company's bill.

YOU AGREE NOT TO
- send messages that break the law where you or the people you write to are;
- collect information from places that do not allow it, or keep contacting
  someone who has asked you to stop;
- use the program to probe websites you do not own for weaknesses;
- publish content that is false, copied, or pretends to be another business;
- use the program for anything illegal.

YOU APPROVE WHAT GOES OUT
An email to a lead, a published page, a change to your website, an offer or a
price waits for your yes. Once you approve something, it is yours.

AI MAKES MISTAKES
The agents' work is written with AI. It can be wrong. Check it before it
reaches anyone else.

UPDATES
Updates are signed by the publisher, and the program installs only an update
whose signature matches. You can go back to the previous version from Settings.
