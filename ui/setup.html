8 · Preflight — test everything at onceEvery brain, every shelf API, every channel — and whether your fallbacks actually work

Testing a primary only tells you today works. This tests every link in every chain, so you find out now — not at 2am when a provider rate-limits — whether losing a primary actually costs you anything. Each result is one of: COVERED (two or more links work, you're safe), SINGLE (only one works — a single point of failure, even if you thought you had a fallback), or DOWN (nothing works).

The full check makes a real, tiny call to every model, so it costs a few tokens. The quick check skips those and only reports what it can verify without calling.
