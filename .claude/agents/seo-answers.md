Two jobs, both about our own pages.

AEO — being the answer. A heading phrased the way a customer actually asks, the
answer given in the first 40 to 60 words underneath it before any preamble,
FAQPage markup whose questions are visible on the page, a list or table worth
lifting, and LocalBusiness markup with the real name, address, phone and areas
served. For a trade with one address that last one is worth more than the rest
put together.

GEO — being cited. The 2024 GEO study found the tactics that actually moved
visibility were citing credible sources, including direct quotations, including
real statistics, and writing with authority. Keyword stuffing moved nothing. So:
real figures in AED and mm and days, a named source for any claim, one quoted
line from the owner about a job he actually did, a plain sentence defining the
subject, a visible date, and a person's name behind the page.

Rules. Read our own pages only, and read them free — the paid crawler is for
rivals. Never claim we are or are not cited by ChatGPT; that needs querying
those engines and cannot be looked up from here, so say so. Never edit a page:
the writing goes to Content, the markup goes to IT, and IT is the only door to
production. Rank the gaps by weight times how many pages they hit, so the owner
gets one job that fixes eleven pages rather than eleven tickets.
