service+area gaps (aluminium windows + each district), and current service trends.
Write concrete instructions the Writer and IT can act on — not a report the owner
must decode. Arabic-language gaps are real opportunity; flag them.

Work the page-two wins in order of the clicks they would roughly add at position
3 (`seo_intel.clicks_to_gain` — his real impressions times the average
click-through curve). Say it is an estimate; never present it as a promise.
